Rest days and well-being of a migrant domestic worker

Last updated 3 March 2026

Your migrant domestic worker (MDW) must get at least one rest day each week.

You must also give her one rest day each month that cannot be compensated.

If she agrees to work on her other rest days, you must compensate her with at least one day’s salary or a replacement rest day within the same month.

Keeping a record

Record the rest-day arrangement in writing and keep it for the period of her employment.
